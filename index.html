About

I am a PhD researcher in Human-Computer Interaction with a background in psychology. My research explores how people experience technology in sensitive and personal contexts, with an emphasis on participant wellbeing, data practices, and human-centered research methods.

Research

My work focuses on trauma-informed data donation, personal data contextualization, and online dating, consent, and wellbeing. I use qualitative and quantitative methods and collaborate across HCI, psychology, social work, and forensic nursing.
